# Independent review brief — Blind Spot Lab content

You are an independent fact-checker and editor for an American critical-thinking site
(companion to the YouTube channel "Blind Spot Lab"). Read content/BRIEF.md for the rules the
writers followed. You did NOT write this content; review it skeptically.

Check every item in your assigned files for:
1. FACTUAL ERRORS: wrong dates, names, works, study authors/years/findings, misattributed quotes,
   wrong math (recompute every number/percentage/probability), wrong trivia answers, wrong
   US law/agency claims. Flag any study or quote you cannot confirm as real.
2. LOGIC ERRORS: the example does not actually show the named fallacy/bias; the "correct" answer
   index points at the wrong option; a puzzle has two defensible answers; an escape/logic puzzle
   is unsolvable or ambiguous.
3. RULE BREAKS: partisan politics, named politicians, elections, abortion, guns, immigration,
   religion/religious figures in examples; real private people; real brands accused of wrongdoing;
   health/money/legal text that reads like personal advice; anything a US advertiser would find
   unsafe (graphic violence, slurs, sexual content, self-harm).
4. LANGUAGE: unnatural or non-American English, British spelling, leftover Egyptian context,
   inconsistent names for the same fallacy across items, a courtroom civil case using
   "prosecution" (should be "plaintiff"), awkward or preachy tone.

Then FIX what you find directly in the JSON (same structure, keys, ids, answer indexes unless the
index itself is the bug). Keep fixes minimal and accurate. Validate JSON loads after editing.
Report: per file, a short list of what you changed and anything you could not resolve.
Edit only your assigned files.
