Dilemma

The Parent Who Needs Care

Your father is getting older and needs care around the clock. You've got a dream job offer in another state, and it won't come around again. Your siblings aren't able to take on the responsibility.

What do you do?

How do philosophers see it?

Ethics of care
Close relationships create special duties. Staying with your father isn't just a practical decision. It's an expression of the relationship itself.
Utilitarianism
If the new job would pay for better professional care and a better future for the whole family, it may be the choice that does more good.
Virtue ethics
Loyalty is a virtue, and pursuing your own potential is also part of a good life. The question is: what balance can you live with, without regret?

There's no single right answer here. The point is knowing why you chose what you chose.
