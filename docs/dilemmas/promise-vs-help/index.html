Dilemma

The Promise and the Emergency

You promised your son you'd be at his school concert today, and he's been looking forward to it all week. On the way, you come across a car accident where someone is hurt and needs a ride to the hospital.

What do you do?

How do philosophers see it?

Conflicting duties (Ross)
The philosopher W. D. Ross said we have many duties, like keeping promises and helping people in need, and when they clash, we have to weigh which one is stronger in that situation.
Utilitarianism
Saving a life or preventing serious injury matters far more than a child's disappointment, even if his disappointment is real.
Ethics of care
Your promise to your son is part of his trust in you. If you decide to help, tell him afterward what happened. That's a bigger lesson in ethics than the concert itself.

There's no single right answer here. The point is knowing why you chose what you chose.
